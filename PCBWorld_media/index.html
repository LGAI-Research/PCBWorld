PCBWorld — Supplementary Videos

Media appendix to the supplementary material (Appendix N, Visualization gallery and LLM failure traces)

The two videos below accompany the case studies in Appendix N of the supplementary material. Each video shows a tool-using LLM agent (GPT-5.4, interactive mode) routing a real production board (D3-A) in PCBWorld. The agent acts step by step through the engine's native routing operations and receives per-operation engine feedback; both episodes end in a successfully routed board.

Video 1 — Case Study 1 board (0018_hy_adapter, Figure 28)

A successful episode on the Case Study 1 board, where the first net-selection decision largely determines the routing outcome.

Video 2 — Case Study 2 board (0100_smt-zvs-driver_IH10-mc, Figure 29)

A successful episode on the Case Study 2 board, where the agent avoids congestion through early-stage planning.
